Historical Context

The MassKara Festival

Bacolod City, Philippines
Origin — 1980. The festival was born out of crisis: plummeting sugar prices and the tragedy of the MV Don Juan sinking. City leaders chose to respond not with grief, but with celebration and unity.

The word MassKara was coined by artist Ely Santiago — a portmanteau of mass (a multitude of people) and the Spanish cara (face), meaning a multitude of faces.

The smiling mask — adorned with feathers, beads, and sequins — has become the enduring symbol of Bacolod's resilience, warmth, and triumphant community spirit.
