Historical Context

The MassKara Festival

Bacolod City · Sugar Capital of the Philippines
EST. 1980
A Triumphant Community Spirit. The festival was born out of crisis: plummeting sugar prices and the tragedy of the MV Don Juan. City leaders and artists chose to respond not with sorrow, but with celebration, resilience, and unified joy.

The word MassKara was coined by artist Ely Santiago — combining mass (a multitude of people) and the Spanish cara (face), creating "a multitude of smiling faces."

👑 Headdress & Feathers Radiant crowns symbolizing soaring hope and artistic pride.
🎭 The Smiling Mask Bacolod's iconic declaration of warmth and enduring positivity.

Every October, Bacolod transforms into a world-renowned spectacle of rhythmic street dancing, electric street parties, vibrant costumes, and delicious local gastronomy.
